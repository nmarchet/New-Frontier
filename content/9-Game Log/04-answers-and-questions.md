# Session 1
## 22-26 Oct. 1864
- As they begin to explore their new powers, the party backtracks through the southern reaches of the Shards. 
- They make their way back to shore of the River Romul, where they reclaim the pole boat the [Syndicate](the-haven-syndicate) agents had stolen (after Himari took their boat).
- Pulling their way against the current this time, the party returns to the Syndicate base at the river's fork.
- Along the way, Ella explores her newfound abilities, especially the detect magic spell. She doesn't find magic―or whatever this phenomenon is―has spread beyond the party.
- Ella also spent time looking through the hidden book she recovered from Issac's office. His copy of *The Midnight Ledger* by Isabelle Durand is well worn. Most pages have a letter, word or short phrase circled. It's clear that the book isn't a message of any kind, but rather it's a cipher for decoding messages.
- They party stops at the base and investigates more thoroughly with the benefit of time, but they don't find much of note―just a couple daggers, three sets of common clothes and 23 stones from the barracks foot lockers.
## 26 Oct. 1864
- One last trip along the river brings the party back to [Kapama](kapama), where there's an odd haze in the air. It's not smoke; it's something grittier, dusty.
- The party returns their well used pole boat and heads into town.
- The town is teeming with refugees who flooded in from the homesteads between [Gendron's Post](gendrons-post) and Kapama when another Scour storm hit. 
- This is the second of these sandstorms to hit within the same month, when the one before that was ten years ago and the first Scour event was 12 years before that. It is the only topic of conversation in the now crowded camps of the Commons. People are scared.
- Listening in on conversations among the refugees throughout the campgrounds, Himari realizes the timing of the Scour event and the party's encounter deep in the Shard mountains lines up pretty darn closely. 
- The party checks the communal animal paddock in the Commons. The hadrosaurs they left there―the ones they rented on Sheriff Drayton's line of credit with Tyrl’s Terrible Lizards in Fairhaven―are gone.
### Erasmus and Elena
- Erasmus and Elena go to the [Panacea](panacea) clinic, where Erasmus' most recent love interest is still the only healer, and the clinic is packed.
- Erasmus decides now is not the time to rekindle this particular romance, and he once again pitches in to help [Nora](nora-pelham) triage and treat the Scour refugees.
- Well into the evening, after the rush of patients have been treated, Erasmus gets a chance to talk with Nora. It quickly becomes clear that she expected Eramus' work to take him … elsewhere, and she's surprised to see him again so soon.
- Elena interjects and attempts to ease the somewhat awkward interaction by turning the conversation toward the party's recent adventures, including the chase deep into the Shards and the desperate midnight fight with the snow monster.
- Elena shows Nora the Panacea pendant that Elena recovered from the mound of dung in the cavern where the Syndicate agents held the beast of Kapama.
- Nora believes is belonged to [Thoren Kael](thoren-kael), who was leading the expedition.
- Ultimately, after hearing all the information Elena and Erasmus had to offer, Nora wanted some time alone to think and write her report. Any follow-up romantic interlude Erasmus might have hoped for was not to be.
### Ella and Himari
- Meanwhile, Ella and Himari find a leather shop, Harlan’s Fine Leathers, and they purchase bandoleers to expand their ammunition storage to 40 rounds total.
- Later, Ella considers stealing hadrosaurs from the communal pen at the Commons, but someone always seems to be watching the paddock.
- Ella is so frustrated by the missing hadrosaurs that she triggers another of her new abilities ([Locate Object](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619002-locate-object)), but the party's former mounts don't appear to be nearby.
## 27 Oct. 1864
- In the morning, Elena buys two sickles and a long bow. Ella looks to buy some ammunition to fill out her new bandoleer, but she is put off by the storekeeper's rather inflexible price.
- Then, the party begins the journey north to Gendron's Post and then Fairhaven.
- Faced with the long, long journey back to Fairhaven on foot, Ella and Elena discover another ability they share ([Find Steed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618879-find-steed)).
- They both summon a hadrosaurus.
- Now mounted, they begin the trip to Gendron's Post in earnest.
## 29 Oct. 1864
- The journey takes longer than the original trip south, because the party need to double up on their newly summoned mounts. Plus, as they travel north, the lands are more and more affected by the Scour. Close to Gendron's Post, whole swaths of the trail are obliterated.
- After a couple days, the party does arrive in Gendron's Post. The small settlement is all but buried. Outside the settlement, dunes of sand have replaced the rolling grassland. In town, the buildings along the street are buried up to their roofs. Even the watchtower is only a few feet above the ground now.
- Only a couple residents remain digging out the settlement one building at a time―[Jane Gendron](jane-gendron); her ward, [Eliot](eliot); and [Marcus Stone](marcus-stone), Ella former intermittent employer and mentor. 
- Ella checks in with Stone while the others head to the watchtower and break the news of Hicks' death to Eliot and Jane.
## 31 Oct. 1864
- In Fairhaven, the party briefly stops at the Settlers’ Rest Inn to freshen up before heading out into the city on their personal business.
### Erastis
- Erastis begins a lover's lament pub crawl. He laments the lack of a troubadour to accompany him and translate his melancholy into song―perhaps a baritone singer/songwriter known for lyrics that explore themes of loneliness, love and outsider feelings.
### Elena
- Elena goes to the Panacea headquarters and finds herself reporting to [Liora Arden](liora-arden), who oversees day-to-day operations for Panacea, for a second time.
- Liora had been aware of the delayed expedition and the staffing shortage. In fact, she'd arranged for a trio of "compadres" to relieve Nora in Kapama. They were currently en route.
- However, when Elena presents the retrieved pendant with a sting of dramatic music and explains exactly where and how it was recovered, the Panacea head recognizes the gravity of the situation.
- She rings a bell on her desk and summons her assistant, a long-suffering woman named Ellen, and tells her to arrange a full cohort of "compadres" to travel to Kapama.
- Liora asks Elena to give her full statement to Ellen, who is also a bit of an artist and does a composite sketch based on her description.
- Afterward, Elena refreshes her medical kit supplies.
### Himari
- Himari checks in on his father's house. Everything is as he left it.
- He starts walking his usual route through the city, and he shifts a certain flower pot on a certain window sill to the left indicating a request to meet with his handler.
- Himari keeps walking looking for the countersign, but after several hours there's still no response.
- Instead, Himari eventually becomes aware that his handler, [Thaddeus](thaddeus-cole), is walking next to him.
- Walking through the streets of Fairhaven, Himari and his handler have an increasingly intense argument about the events in Kapama and the disruption of a critical client's operation―all because of some outside work. Thaddeus once again insists that it's time for Himari to pick a side.
- Himari accuses the Haven Syndicate of killing his father. 
- Thaddeus doesn't seem to have any knowledge of that, but he says, "I don't know what evidence you think you have. What I do know is that someone who is accusing the Syndicate of these things does not have any place in the Syndicate. And that has implications for someone who has worked for us as long as you have."
- Thaddeus tells Himari to take some time to think about what he really wants to say. He stops and catches Himari's eyes and says, "You really want to think about this."
- Himari responds, "Careful what you wish for," and triggers two of his new abilities. His eyes glow with a [Minor Illusion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619039-minor-illusion) and then he disappears from the middle of the busy street with [Misty Step](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619133-misty-step).
### Ella
- Ella makes her down toward the docks and the shipyards, where it looks like the first ship of the Fairhaven colonies looks pretty much ready to launch.
- As she arrives, there's no smoke coming from Mara's smithy, which is unusual. In the brief time Ella knew Mara, she would never let the forage go cold.
- Ella draws a revolver and steps into the smithy.
- The orderly shop Ella remembers is a disaster. She can't tell whether there's been a fight or it's just been tossed.
