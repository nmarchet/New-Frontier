# Session 1
## 19 Oct. 1864
### Erastis, Elena and Ella
- In the cave behind the Syndicate expedition base on the Whakatāne frontier the party finds and kills an enormous beast, which was seemingly responsible for the deaths around Kapama.
- In the aftermath of the battle, in which Hick was eaten whole, Ella drops to her knees nearly catatonic.
- Erastis consoles Ella and gets her on her feet.
- As the party prepares to make a hasty exit, Elena notices a glint of something shiny in one of the prodigious piles of feces in the cavern. 
- She pulls out a pendant―a stylize sprig of herbs crossed with a feather, the emblem of Panacea. It looks like at least one of the members of the missing Panacea survey team died here.
- The party makes its way out of the cavern, back around the the knob toward the Syndicate base. There's no warning smoke signal from Himari visible.
- The path back to the base is clear, and there are no guards around.
- Still wary, the party slips of the path as they head back to the boat landing on the banks of River Romul.
### Himari
- Meanwhile, a mile or so to the south, Himari jumps off the stolen Syndicate boat he's run aground and begins running back up-river to rejoin his companions.
### Erastis, Elena and Ella
- Erastis, Elena, Ella and Hicks pass the macabre aftermath of Himari's encounter at the watchtower and reach the boat landing. There is no boat.
- Remembering Himari planned to go a little ways down-river to prepare a signal fire, Erastis notices the smell of smoke though there's still no smoke visible.
- Following the the smell of smoke, the party finds the remnants of Himari's signal fire. It's been extinguished, and the pole boat the party took from Kapama is nowhere to be seen. However, there are five fish hanging from a nearby branch.
- Momentarily at a loss, the party rests, and Ella investigates the documents she retrieved from Isaac/Pedro's office. She learns:
	- Pedro is actually named Isaac; he seems to be a relatively high-level Syndicate operative; and, importantly, he's not dead.
	- Isaac seems to have established his relationship with Ella as a way to spy on her family, trying to uncover the secrets to the unusually bountiful crops that no other homesteaders seemed to be able to match.
	- Isaac coordinated the "bandit attack"―really, a Syndicate hit squad―that killed Ella's family.
	- Himari is mentioned in correspondence as a potentially valuable asset if he can be brought fully into the Syndicate.
	- The letter also alludes to Himari's father, Daisuke, who was once a valuable operative who later objected to Syndicate work and was killed.
	- The annotated map of the Syndicate's expeditions has a fourth expedition penciled in that largely follows the route of the the second mission into the Shards. The note reads, "Second expedition may not have been a complete loss. Carson Grundy’s return last month―even raving as he was―suggests something valuable may be waiting for us.” Then, there are individual phrases in quotation marks: “forgotten paths,” “the sacred fire,” and “signs hidden in shadows.”
	- And, generally, a long-term plan of the Syndicate comes into focus. The organization seems intent on gathering flora, fauna and goods that are unique to Whakatāne in an effort to broker some grand business deal. However, the details of this deal―who, why and when―remain unknown.
### Himari
- About this time, Himari is still running along the river―a little more than halfway back to the Syndicate base.
- He sees a familiar boat coming down the river, the party's pole boat. However, now it is fully loaded with supplies and about eight Syndicate agents―including the leader Himari saw earlier at the landing.
### Erastis, Elena and Ella
- Finding their boat gone and the camp seemingly abandoned, the party decides to go back to the base to see what they can find to help them navigate the river.
- In the back storage room of the Syndicate base, the party finds a great deal of supplies have been taken. The missing gear aligns with the expedition checklist Erastis had received earlier.
- There is still plenty of equipment available―rations, tents, sleeping bags, cold weather gear, climbing apparatus and more―as well as a couple dusty canoes hanging on the back wall.
- The group kits themselves out with all the gear from the expedition list, including an extra set for Himari (assuming he's still alive). They take the canoes as well.
- On the way out of the base, Ella searches Pedro's/Isaac's office one more time.
- She finds a loose wall board, behind which is a book titled *The Midnight Ledger* by Isabelle Durand.
- Upon brief inspection, the books is one of those trashy romance novels Pedro used to favor. The story is seemingly about an assassin working for one of the great families of Rosslyn who falls in love with the opposing agent he was sent to kill.
- However, flipping through, Ella sees that some letters are underlined. The book is a cypher―either that or it contains an encoded message of some kind itself.
### Together again
- As the others are leaving the Syndicate base as well equipped as they've ever been, Himari bursts into the small clearing around his signal fire. 
- Someone has put the fire out, and the pole boat is missing. Although, the trout are still hanging from the tree.
- Himari takes a fish and heads to the boat landing.
- No one is there, so Himari walks to the Syndicate base, where he meets the rest of the group walking out carrying canoes over their heads.
- They exchange information:
	- Ella reveals the identity of her (still living!) husband and explains the Syndicate's role in the death of Himari's father. 
	- Erastis breaks the news about the untimely consumption of Hicks by the beast.
	- Elena shares the apparent fate of at least some of the missing Panacea survey.
	- Himari is able to fill in the blanks about where the Syndicate agents went. By the time the party gets the canoes down to the river, loaded and ready to launch, he estimates they have at least an hour's head start.
- The party launches the canoes, with Himari and Erastis in the lead boat followed by Ella and Elana. They head even further south down River Romul in pursuit of Ella's husband. 
- Himari and Erastis race ahead and find the Syndicate boat that Himari grounded. 
- Erastis thinks this might be a faster boat, but with camp shovels, they pair hardly make any progress digging the boat out of the sandbar in which it is grounded.
- So, the group continues by canoe.
- By nightfall, they have paddled for miles, aided by the river's current.
- Moving so quickly, they almost miss their original pole boat. It has been pulled up onto the western shore and covered with branches to hide it.
- The party lands. Making the most of the fading light, they follow tracks across the plains toward the Shards.
- Suddenly, Ella is clipped in the shoulder, spun around and knocked to the ground. There's the delayed crack of a rifle shot―a sniper has been left behind somewhere ahead.
- After a minute of waiting, Ella runs toward the general direction of the shooter. After another shot misses, she has a solid bead on the sniper's perch.
- Himari and Erastis follow with more tactical approaches. Elena follows behind. She expects her medical skills will be needed soon.
- The sniper continues firing, hitting Himari multiple times. As they close within revolver range, they start returning fire.
- But, Erastis gets there first. He sneaks up behind the sniper and smashed him with the haft of his handaxe.
- Ella arrives shortly after and shoots the Syndicate agent dead.
- The party decides to camp for the night, confident they'll be able to pick up the trail again in the morning.
- They don't light a fire, and it's quite a cold night, but it passes with no further violence.
## 20 Oct. 1864
- In the morning, they see the Shards are quite close now. By midday, they are very much in the foothills.
- By evening, the trail left by the Syndicate agents takes the party into the mountains proper.
- Around the camp that evening, the discuss the letter Ella found that mentions Himari and his father and the arrangements for the murder of Ella's family.
- Erastis and Himari recognize the names―and even worked with some―of the agents assigned to that mission.
- They don't know the letter's signatory, Malcolm, for sure. However, they do know there is a Malcolm Graves, who is a senior partner at the Haven Syndicate. He oversees logistics and strategic planning for the Syndicate’s protection services.
- The night passes without incident.
## 21 Oct. 1864
- The trail of the Syndicate agents takes the party high up among the mountain peaks. The cold weather gear they brought is essential in these snow-covered elevations.
- Around midday, the party is navigating a broad ledge along a cliff when they find signs of a violent fight. There are blood splatters, frozen pools of gore and marks in the snow that indicate at least two people were dragged away by something walking on two clawed feet.
- Alert for danger, the party nevertheless presses ahead.
- That evening, the party finds an overhang in the cliff face for some partial shelter. There's enough snow on the ground they're able to dig down a couple feet to light a fire and keep it obscured from view.
- Erastis takes the first watch. A mound of snow moves nearby, but Erastis doesn't see it until it's looming over him―a towering creature, over ten feet tall. It's covered in shaggy, snow-white hair, with curling horns and piercing blue eyes.
- Erastis shouts in alarm and manages to wake the party as the beast lunges toward him.
- The creature's blue eyes lock on Erastis. Under it's chilling gaze, Erastis freezes. He can't move.
- The creature turns to the rest of the party. Despite Erastis' warning, the others are soon badly injured with gouges from its claws and a freezing breath that drops Ella and Elana, leaving Himari facing the creature alone.
- Elena stirs, and Erastis comes back to himself and rejoins the fight. 
- Between them, Erastis and Himari manage to kill the creature, with Erastis finishing it off with a powerful kick and Himari rolling aside just in time to avoid being crushed as falls.
- The party regroups, heals and finishes the night without further incident.
- Ella decides to call the creature a "yeti."
## 22 Oct. 1864
- In the morning, it's hard to pick up the path again. When they do, there is only sign of one person passing.
- Near midday, Ella, who is leading the group, plunges through the snow and out of sight without warning.
- She fall 50-60 feet down a rough, icy slope into a dark cavern.
- Erastis secures a line and climbs down after Ella.
- Down with Ella, Erastis feels a faint warm breeze coming from further down in the cavern, and his ears strain unconsciously―grasping at whispers of something he can't quite catch.
- Himari follows, and eventually Elena joins the group in the cavern below.
- Erastis lights a torch. The cavern in which the party finds itself is more like crevasse, like something pushed its way through the earth and was later capped with snow.
- As Himari is looking around, he thinks back the the quotes from Carson Grundy written on the margin of the Syndicate map―"“forgotten paths,” “signs hidden in shadows,” “the sacred fire.” 
- As he thinks about that last line, Himari notices something. some undulation in the light. It's too much to even call it light, but there's some sort of change in darkness and light further down the crevasse that is not explained by the torch Erastis is holding.
- Himari scouts ahead, tied to one of the party's ropes.
- Just past the end of his tether, the crevasse turns from view. Himari can see very dim, but definite, red and yellow pulses in the light ahead. 
- After some debate about the wisdom of proceeding further, the party decides to continue on.
- The group links their ropes together, with Erastis using pitons to secure each subsequent line.
- After one last final curve, the crevasse opens up into a larger room, where the source of the undulating lights stand: three people in the center of the room.
- They're larger than regular folks, almost as tall as the "yeti." It's two women surrounded by yellow auras reaching toward a single man between them, who has a pulsing red light washing around him. Aside from the the pulsing light, the three seem frozen in this scene.
- The room is absolutely filled with oddly warm air and the unarticulated whispers just beyond comprehension that Erastis first noticed earlier.
- On the floor, about 15 feet away from the trio is a human-sized body collapsed in a heap. Using the spyglass taken from the Syndicate safe house, Himari and Ella are able to identify the body as Pedro/Isaac.
- Ella tosses out a rope and grapple to try to pull Pedro to them, but he gets hung up on a rock about 10 feet away. 
- Elena can tell he's still breathing, so Himari steps forward into the room to drag Pedro out.
- As he stands over Isaac, the whispers become clear to Himari―not words per se, but the emotions behind them. When the light flashes red he is almost overwhelmed by a sense of violent rage. When the yellow aura is dominant, the whispers a kind and beseeching, almost desperate to help.
- Caught up in the whispers, Himari steps over Pedro's body and walks toward one of the women surrounded in yellow. He reaches out and touches that border between light and not light, and everything erupts into motion.
- There is a blast of warmth as first yellow energy, then red energy fill the room―swirling, whirling around, touching everything in the room, permeating every*one*. 
- The figures in the scene are moving too. The two women lunge toward the central figure, who throws them off with wild haymakers.
- Then they vanish, leaving the light behind. The yellow light pours into Himari, Erastis, Elena and Ella. The red light pushes into Pedro, but then shoots out of him. It races back down the crevasse and out of sight.
# Session 2
## 22 Oct. 1864 (continued)
- As the yellow light fills Himari, Erastis, Elena and Ella, each member of the party feels a voice inside them. It asks three questions.
	- What is your strength?
	- What is your weakness?
	- What is your heart?
- As the last traces of inner luminescence fade, the party discovers they all seem to have some strange new abilities.
- While experimenting with a new magical sight ([Detect Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619097-detect-magic)), Ella notices some crystals embedded here and there in the rock of the crevasse and points them out to the group.
- Himari is able to chip out one. It's about an inch wide as its widest and shines slightly sea green with some internal light.
- While Himari chips out the odd gem, Ella gathers the rest of the group and convinces them to behead and burn the body of Pedro/Isaac.
- As the body burns, the group heads out for the long trek down from the mountains.
