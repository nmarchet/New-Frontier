**Faction/Organization:** [Panacea](panacea)<br>
**Role:** Healer<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Nora Pelham is a healer at the Panacea clinic in Kapama. Originally built as a small research outpost and treatment center, the clinic now also serves as Kapama’s primary source of medical care. Nora usually remains as the sole healer on duty when the other practitioners and students embark on expeditions.

**Personality:** Nora is warm and compassionate with a strong sense of duty and empathy. Those who get to know her will find a resilient spirit and a dry wit. She values community and connection. She appreciates the bonds she forms with the people of Kapama and cherishes the moments of camaraderie and laughter.

**Appearance:** Nora is a 30-year-old woman with shoulder-length brown hair pulled back in a loose pony tail. She has tired hazel eyes with dark circles under them. She wears simple dark clothes and an apron with a satchel around her waist.
