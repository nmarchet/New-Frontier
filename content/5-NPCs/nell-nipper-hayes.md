**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Pickpocket, childhood friend of [Erastis Freyn](erastis-freyn)<br>
**Primary Location:** [Fairhaven](fairhaven)

Nell is one of many children who were left to fend for themselves as everyone in the new colony struggled for survival. She grew up on the streets of Fairhaven, part of a gang of street kids who survived through pickpocketing, second-story work and the occasional mugging. When her friend Erastis life was recruited into the Haven Syndicate (the "Shades" in street parlance), Nell continued to live by her wits on the streets

**Personality:** Nell is independent. She’s been hardened by years of day-to-day survival, but she soft spot for other street kids. She'll often share what little she has with them. She’s a chatterbox, prone to talking her way into and out of trouble.

**Appearance:** Average height with short, choppy brown hair tucked under a cap. Her face is thin and weathered. Nell dresses in a patched wool vest over a threadbare shirt, breeches and scuffed leather boots.
