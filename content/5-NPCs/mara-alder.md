**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Blacksmith<br>
**Primary Location:** [Fairhaven](fairhaven)

As a young blacksmith, Mara Alder worked in the House Velyra forges, producing swords, spears and armor. When war came, she was devastated by the destruction her work caused—including the deaths of family and friends. Mara realized Lord Velyra and the other great families had all expected war eventually, and she had been part of making that belief a reality. She vowed never to forge another weapon.

Exiled to Whakatāne, she embraced the chance for a fresh start. Her focus now is on building tools that sustain her community, using her craft to aid in shipbuilding, agriculture and daily life.

Mara is in her seventies now. But in those years, she's only taken one apprentice, [Marcus Stone](marcus-stone). He's the only one who Mara got along with and who complied with her prohibition against forging weapons without complaint.

**Personality:** Mara is guarded these days, slow to trust. But, she's fiercely protective of those she's let in her circle. Those who know her value her advice however bluntly it comes.

**Appearance:** Mara is old―very old―and you can see every one of those years on her face. But, she's definitely not weak. In fact, visitors can see individual muscles in her arm move as she hammers. She keeps her grey hair tied back. She wears a heavy leather apron over denim overalls.
