**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Boy about town<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Eliot is a Scour orphan. [Jane Gendron](jane-gendron) found him as a two- to three-year-old in the aftermath of the second Scour event in year 31 (1855 DE). He's been living with her ever since.

**Personality:** Eliot has grown up right along side of Gendron's Post. He is fiercely loyal to Jane, but he wants desperately to be independent and not to rely on anyone. He is relentlessly mercenary when it comes to money in the way that only a 12-year-old can be. He hopes to buy himself passage off Whakatāne someday on a [Wayfinders' Union](the-wayfinders-union) ship.

**Appearance:** Eliot is a slim young boy with tousled, sandy brown hair that he frequently brushes back from large, brown eyes. He wears faded corduroy and a denim shirt. He carries a small satchel slung cross-body and keeps a slingshot tucked in his belt at the small of his back.





**Description**: Emmett Harlow, known as “Grizzly” by the locals, is a broad-shouldered, barrel-chested man in his late 50s with a bushy gray beard and a perpetually furrowed brow. His nickname comes from both his imposing size and his habit of growling under his breath when things don’t go according to plan. He’s a no-nonsense, straightforward man with a deep, gravelly voice and a protective attitude toward his grain stores. Emmett wears a heavy leather apron and gloves, even when he’s not working, and he’s rarely seen without his wide-brimmed hat, which has protected him from sun, rain, and dust over the years.

**Background**: Emmett has managed the granary near Kapama for nearly two decades, overseeing the storage and distribution of the colony’s grain. He’s a reliable figure in the community and known for his vigilance, especially after a recent break-in that made him tighten security around the granary. Though he’s a traditionalist, he agreed to electrify the barbed wire around the granary after the break-in, working alongside Ivor to implement the new security measure. Grizzly is hesitant about technology but trusts Ivor’s expertise and is determined to protect the grain stores from further theft.
