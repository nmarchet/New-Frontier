**Faction/Organization:** Kapama Farmers’ Collective<br>
**Role:** Manager for the Grainery<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

For nearly two decades Emmett Harlow has managed the grain storage and distribution center (known colloquially as the Grainery) for the Kapama Farmers’ Collective, which negotiates standard rates for the settlement’s produce. He also stocks and guards the collective’s seed library. After a recent break-in, he's been working with [Ivor Nyberg](ivor-nyberg) a [Wayfinders' Union](the-wayfinders-union) engineer, to "electrify" the barbed wire fence around the granary.

**Personality:** Emmett is a no-nonsense man, known as a reliable figure in the community.

**Appearance:** Emmett is a broad-shouldered man in his late 50s with a bushy gray beard and a perpetually furrowed brow. He wears a heavy leather apron and gloves, even when he’s not working, and he’s rarely seen without his wide-brimmed hat, which has protected him from sun, rain, and dust over the years.
