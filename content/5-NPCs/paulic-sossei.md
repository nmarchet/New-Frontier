**Faction/Organization:** [The Haven Syndicate](the-haven-syndicate)<br>
**Role:** Role<br>
**Primary Location:** [Fairhaven](fairhaven)

Paulic Sossei is a rising star in the Haven Syndicate, specializing in risk management. He oversees a team of operatives who provide security for high-value targets and assets, manage sensitive operations, and mitigate potential threats to the Syndicate's interests. Paulic reports directly to senior partner [Malcolm Graves](malcolm-graves). 

**Personality:** Paulic has a calm and collected demeanor, but he is understood to be ambitious and driven, seeking to climb the ranks of the Syndicate. He is highly observant and a skilled negotiator.

**Appearance:** Paulic is a man of average height and build, with dark, short-cropped hair and soft blue eyes. He typically dresses in plain, but well-tailored suits, favoring dark colors and subtle accessories.
