**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Traveling merchant<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Anya Greer is a traveling merchant. Currently, she is set up at the Commons, some fields outside of Kapama where visitors and workers who can't afford better accommodations park their wagon or set up camp.

**Personality:** Anya is professionally pleasant, and most conversations somehow veer into a sales pitch. However, for those who manage to get past her saleswoman facade, she's open, kind and funny. 

**Appearance:** Anya has long blonde hair and green eyes. She favors practical attire suitable for travel. She wears a wide belt at her waist, a simple pendant and small hoop earrings.
