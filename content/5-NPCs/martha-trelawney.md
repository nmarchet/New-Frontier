**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Owner of the Settlers' Rest Inn<br>
**Primary Location:** [Fairhaven](fairhaven)

Martha Trelawney is the proprietor of the Settlers' Rest Inn. She inherited it from her father who help build several buildings in the new settlement including this one. She seems to have a longstanding relationship with [Sheriff Mariah Drayton](mariah-drayton).

**Personality:** Martha's genuine kindness and compassion create a welcoming atmosphere, making the inn a true haven for travelers and locals alike. She's always ready to listen or offer a comforting word to those who seek it.

**Appearance:** Martha is tall and broad-shouldered with a round and open face. She has dark hair streaked with silver that she wears up. She favors comfortable clothing in cheerful colors.
