**Role:** Amateur herbalist, showman<br>
**Primary Location:** Unknown

Dr. Ezekiel Horne is not a doctor; he was a young newspaper man back in Rosslyn. Like many reporters, he had a side job with one of the great families. He was a spy and propagandist for House Velyra, and he was swept up in the brief civil war and subsequent exile of that family.

In Whakatāne, Horne worked as a forager in [Glendale](glendale). He discovered a tree with mottled yellow-black bark. He found the bark was a mild narcotic; when chewed, it offered some mild pain relief. Horne left Glendale and set himself up as Dr. Ezekiel Horne with a traveling medicine show to sell his new Miracle Tonic made from the tree’s bark.

Most recently, Horne was seen fleeing [Gendron's Post](gendrons-post) on the road to [Sand Lake](sand-lake) after his assistant, Lila Marsh. 

**Personality:** Horne is charismatic and quick-witted. Beneath his charm, he is a cunning opportunist, always seeking the next chance to advance his own interests, even if it means bending the truth.

**Appearance:** Horne is a man in his late 50s, though his confidence makes him seem younger. He has brown hair that's a touch unkempt from his travels and a full, neatly trimmed beard. He wears a faded suit and  top hat. Horne's hazel eyes twinkle with a mix of mischief and ambition.
