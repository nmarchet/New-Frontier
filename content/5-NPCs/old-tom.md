**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Ferryman<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Old Tom is a notorious drunkard. He's also the ferryman, who pulls a tethered ferry―a large raft, really―down the Vein River from just outside Kapama to the Twin River Bridge, where the caravans that move goods to and from Glendale meet up.

**Personality:** Old Tom is easy-going and talkative. Even drunk―especially drunk―he will go on at length about the craft of the ferryman. 

**Appearance:** Old Tom is a middle-aged man, with long dirty blonde hair and a bushy beard. He mostly stares off into the middle distance, but his eyes get intense and focused when talking about the craft of the ferryman. Despite his habitual drinking, he's relatively tidy. Old Tom usually wears slightly rumpled work clothes.
