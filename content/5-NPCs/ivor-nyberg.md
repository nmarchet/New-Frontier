**Faction/Organization:** N/A<br>
**Role:** Boy about town<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Eliot is a Scour orphan. [Jane Gendron](jane-gendron) found him as a two- to three-year-old in the aftermath of the second Scour event in year 31 (1855 DE). He's been living with her ever since.

**Personality:** Eliot has grown up right along side of Gendron's Post. He is fiercely loyal to Jane, but he wants desperately to be independent and not to rely on anyone. He is relentlessly mercenary when it comes to money in the way that only a 12-year-old can be. He hopes to buy himself passage off Whakatāne someday on a [Wayfinders' Union](the-wayfinders-union) ship.

**Appearance:** Eliot is a slim young boy with tousled, sandy brown hair that he frequently brushes back from large, brown eyes. He wears faded corduroy and a denim shirt. He carries a small satchel slung cross-body and keeps a slingshot tucked in his belt at the small of his back.







**Description**: Ivor Nyberg is a seasoned engineer from the Wayfinders’ Union, known for his calm demeanor and practical approach to even the most complicated problems. In his mid-40s, Ivor has short-cropped, graying hair and sharp blue eyes that seem to measure and calculate everything around him. He’s tall and wiry, with a slight stoop from years of hunching over drafting tables and machinery. His hands are calloused and nicked from working with tools, and he wears a pair of round spectacles that he keeps pushing up his nose when he’s focused.

**Background**: Originally from a bustling industrial city in Meredosia, Ivor has spent years as an engineer for the Wayfinders’ Union, helping design and repair their ships and equipment. His specialty lies in adapting old technology to meet new demands, making him an ideal candidate for converting the windmill outside Kapama. He has a wry sense of humor and is meticulous in his work, often muttering jargon to himself as he troubleshoots problems. Though he’s a bit of a perfectionist, he’s not above sharing his knowledge with locals, especially if it will make their lives easier or safer.
