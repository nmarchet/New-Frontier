**Faction/Organization:** [Wayfinders’ Union](the-wayfinders-union.md)<br>
**Role:** Engineer<br>
**Primary Location:** [Kapama](kapama)

Ivor Nyberg is a seasoned engineer from the Wayfinders’ Union. Back in Meredosia, Ivan was recruited by the Union right out of school―the University of Aubrey in Cresca, Manton. He has spent years as an engineer, helping design and repair their ships and equipment. Currently, he is converting the windmill outside Kapama to generate electricity as part of the Union's treaty with the People’s Council.

**Personality:** Ivor has a wry sense of humor and is meticulous in his work, often muttering jargon to himself as he troubleshoots problems. Though he’s a bit of a perfectionist, he’s not above sharing his knowledge.

**Appearance:**  Ivor is in his mid-40s, with short graying hair and sharp blue eyes. He’s tall and wiry, with a slight stoop from years of hunching over drafting tables and machinery. His hands are calloused and nicked from working with tools, and he wears a pair of round spectacles that he absentmindedly pushes up his nose when he’s focused on a problem.
