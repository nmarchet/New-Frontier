The Scour is the name folks have given to the extreme winds that occasionally drive down from the Shards and tear across the plains and desert regions south of Fairhaven. These powerful winds turn loose dust and debris into a deadly and prolonged abrasive blast.
![[the-scour-descends.jpg]]
# Stage 1—Sandstorm (30-50 mph winds)

- Disadvantage on ranged attacks.
- Area is [Lightly Obscured](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#LightlyObscured).
	- Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.
- Area is [Difficult Terrain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#DifficultTerrain).
	- Every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.
- When moving:
	- Small-sized players must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw to avoid falling prone.
# Stage 2—Flensing Sandstorm (50-75 mph winds)
- Ranged attacks are impossible.
- Area is [Heavily Obscured](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#HeavilyObscured) 
	- Players have the [Blinded](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#BlindedCondition) condition.
		- They automatically fail any ability check that requires sight. 
		- Attack rolls against them have Advantage.
		- Player attack rolls have Disadvantage.
- Area is [Difficult Terrain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#DifficultTerrain).
	- Every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.
- When moving:
	- Small-sized players must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw to avoid falling prone and being knocked back 10 feet.
    - Medium-sized players must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw to avoid falling prone.
- Sand does 1d4 slashing damage per round to all unprotected players.
# Stage 3—Scour (75-150 mph winds) 
- Ranged attacks are impossible. 
- Area is [Heavily Obscured](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#HeavilyObscured) 
	- Players have the [Blinded](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#BlindedCondition) condition.
		- They automatically fail any ability check that requires sight. 
		- Attack rolls against them have Advantage.
		- Player attack rolls have Disadvantage.
- Area is [Difficult Terrain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#DifficultTerrain).
	- Every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.   
- When moving:
	- Both small- and medium-sized players must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw to avoid falling prone and being knocked back 10 feet.
- Sand does 1d8 slashing damage per round to all unprotected players.
